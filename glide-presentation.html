Product Concept

What problem does Glide solve?

Traditional EMI requires credit cards (16–18% interest) or CIBIL scores. Glide replaces that with your mutual fund portfolio as collateral.

💳

Old Way: Credit Card EMI

16–18% p.a. interest, credit score inquiry, hidden processing fees, foreclosure penalties.

🚀

Glide: Mutual Fund EMI

0% interest up to 24 months, zero CIBIL impact, your portfolio keeps compounding.

📈

Funds Stay Invested

Digital lien on liquid fund units. Dividends and NAV growth continue crediting to your account.

Architecture Decisions

Backend vs Frontend Demo Split

✅ Real Backend (Neon PostgreSQL)
  • ✓Products, variants & EMI plans from live DB
  • ✓Category & keyword search with parameterized SQL
  • ✓In-memory TTL cache on Express (30s)
  • ✓Chatbot rule engine on server
  • ✓CORS, 404/500 handlers, health check
📦 Frontend-only (Clearly Labeled)
  • ✓Cart state → React Context + localStorage
  • ✓Checkout → mock pledge simulation, no real payment
  • ✓Testimonials & FAQ → static marketing copy
  • ✓EMI Calculator → client-side math only
Deployment-ready:
Backend → Render/Railway (set DATABASE_URL)
Frontend → Vercel (set VITE_API_BASE_URL)
